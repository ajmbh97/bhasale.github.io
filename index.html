How I Lead Clinical Data Management

The vision

Across the portfolio, clinical data the company can act on at every milestone: interim, lock, topline, filing.

Reliable, decision-ready data behind every major program decision.

Decision-ready

Data planned around every milestone that depends on it, through to submission.

Inspection-ready

Quality built in so audits and inspections confirm controls that are already in place.

Built to scale

A focused in-house team with the right mix of internal and partner capacity.

Strategic governance plan so outsourced work stays on time and on quality.

Shared insights

One integrated, near real-time view of the data, built on BI and AI tools.

My Data Superpowers 🦸‍♀️

The skills I have mastered to fight the forces of messy data and inefficient processes

Leadership

Building and mentoring high-performing global CDM teams, fostering collaboration across functions, and creating a culture of continuous improvement and innovation.

Data Strategy

Developing comprehensive data strategies aligned with clinical development objectives, ensuring data quality, integrity, and regulatory compliance throughout the drug development lifecycle.

Regulatory Compliance

Ensuring adherence to FDA, EMA, ICH-GCP, and other regulatory requirements, with expertise in CDISC standards (C-DASH, SDTM, ADaM, ODM), 21 CFR Part 11, and data privacy regulations.

Therapeutic Expertise

Deep understanding of oncology, rare diseases, immunology, inflammatory pain, hepatology and cardiovascular therapeutics, with ability to tailor data strategies to specific therapeutic area requirements.

Process Optimization

Implementing lean methodologies and digital solutions to streamline clinical data processes, reduce cycle times, and improve operational efficiency without compromising quality.

Data Analytics

Leveraging advanced analytics, machine learning, gen AI, and visualization techniques to derive actionable insights from clinical trial data, supporting evidence-based decision making.
